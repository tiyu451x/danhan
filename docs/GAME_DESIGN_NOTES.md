# Madiun Memory — Design Direction

## Recommended core loop

Explore → notice something wrong → find a memory card → insert it → enter a historical scenario → solve a short problem → learn one real historical fact → recover from the scenario → unlock a new route/card.

The historical fact should be part of the reason the player acts, not merely a paragraph shown after the fun is over.

## Gameplay recommendations

1. Make the chase short. Your Subway Surfers-like first-person sequence works best as a punctuation mark: roughly 20–60 seconds in an early prototype.
2. Give each historical scenario one unique mechanic. A railway scenario can use timing/routing; a municipal scenario can use document sorting; an industrial scenario can use logistics/matching.
3. Make the card the player’s mental model: where/when are we, what changed, what must be recovered, and why it matters.
4. Make mistakes alter the digital world. A wrong puzzle answer can increase corruption, alter dialogue, or add a small complication instead of always showing “GAME OVER.”
5. Reward curiosity rather than grinding. Hide optional observations, historical details, image comparisons, and environmental interactions.
6. Keep RNG outside the critical history path. Randomness can choose optional cards, side scenarios, cosmetic variants, or replay modifiers; the core educational route should stay deterministic.
7. Make the material mode part of the loop. After a scenario, the player can open the real-world material behind it and inspect sources, maps, images, and a timeline.
8. Treat the monsters as symbolic corrupted information rather than simply saying “social media is bad.” The threat can represent forgotten memory, algorithmic noise, repetition, and historical erasure.
9. Make the dream ending re-contextualize the story rather than erase it. Plant details earlier that make the player question what is real.
10. Do not build a large combat system first. Chase + exploration + dialogue + card + puzzle already proves the concept.

## First scenario candidates

### 1918 — The city becomes a municipality
The official city history says Stadsgemeente/Kota Praja Madiun was established on 20 June 1918. Build a scenario around the city changing administrative form.

### 1930 — The Raadhuis
The city government says the building now known as Balai Kota Madiun, formerly Raadhuis te Madioen, was opened on 1 August 1930. This is visually strong for architecture, documents, city officials, and place-based exploration.

### 1894 — PG Rejo Agung
The city government dates the beginning of PG Rejo Agung to 1894 and connects it to the growth of the sugar economy. This can be turned into a scenario about industrial change, transport, labor, and the city’s economic network.

Verify all historical claims against authoritative sources before the public release.
